Kwagi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Kwagi is an offline study app. It does not require an account and does not send your study data to a Kwagi server.

Data stored on your device

Kwagi stores your profile, subjects, notes, flashcards, quiz history, study progress, and app preferences locally on your device. This data supports the app's study features.

Backups

Kwagi only exports data when you choose to create a backup. On Android, the system share sheet lets you choose where to send the backup. On the web, your browser downloads a JSON file. Any destination you choose has its own privacy policy.

Data collection and sharing

This release has no advertising, analytics, account system, cloud sync, payment processing, or AI service. Kwagi does not sell personal data.

Deleting data

Use Settings > Clear all data to remove your notes, cards, quiz history, progress, and preferences. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may also remove local data. Android system backup is disabled for Kwagi. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Children

Kwagi is intended for college students and general study use. It is not directed to children under 13.

Policy changes

Material changes will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Support

For privacy questions or deletion help, use the Kwagi support page.
