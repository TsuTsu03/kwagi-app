Kwagi Support

Kwagi stores study data on your device. Support cannot remotely view or recover that data.

Before reporting a problem

  1. Close and reopen Kwagi.
  2. Confirm that your device has free storage.
  3. Export a backup before clearing data or reinstalling.

Report an issue

Open a Kwagi support request on GitHub. Do not include private notes, backup files, passwords, or other personal data.

What to include

Include your device model, operating-system version, Kwagi version, the screen where the problem happened, and steps that reproduce it.
