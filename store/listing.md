## Google Play copy

Short description (79/80): Turn class notes into flashcards and quizzes, then review offline at your pace.

Full description:

Study your own notes with flashcards, spaced repetition, and practice quizzes. Kwagi works offline and keeps your study library on your device.

Organize subjects, write notes, turn structured note lines into flashcards, review cards when they are due, and build multiple-choice quizzes from your own card library.

Your study library stays on your device. Kwagi does not require an account and this release has no ads, analytics, payments, cloud sync, or AI service. You can export a backup whenever you want a copy you control.

Kwagi supports:

- Notes grouped by subject
- Manual cards and note-to-card generation
- Spaced-repetition reviews with Again, Hard, Good, and Easy ratings
- Multiple-choice quizzes made from your active cards
- Daily goals, streaks, XP, and progress history
- Light, dark, and device themes
- Device Reduce Motion support
- Offline use after installation

Kwagi organizes study material that you create. It does not replace instructors, official course materials, or professional advice.
